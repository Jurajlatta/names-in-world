Want a house?

  1. Find @names_in_world on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube.
  2. Comment house under any video, any time.
  3. Within 24 hours your name is on this map, with your number.

    The rules

    First comment gets the lowest number, and your number never changes. One house per account. 16+ only.

    We show your account's username as it is, and we read every name before it goes up. Offensive names stay off the map.

    Want out? Comment remove or message @names_in_world. Your name comes off this map within 24 hours and stays out of future videos. The number is retired, nobody else gets it.
